What it does

When your insurance company emails about your claim - like the adjustment date or the estimate - a copy goes to your Heirloom Roofing rep automatically.

Copies are sent by our service address, aidanleavitt.ops@gmail.com - never from your address - and go only to Aidan's roofing inbox at that same address.

What you'll see next

  • A Google sign-in screen for a connection service called UNIPILE.
  • Google shows checkboxes - this setup only needs "View your email messages and settings". Leave the others unchecked.
That read permission covers your whole mailbox - Google doesn't offer a claims-only option. The connection service can read your mailbox, but this setup relays only email that arrives after you connect and matches your claim: your insurance company and claim details, which you confirm during setup. Older email and non-matching email are not relayed. You approve it yourself, and you can say no.

Prefer not to connect?

No problem - just forward claim emails to your rep when they arrive.

You can turn this off later in your Google account under Security > Third-party access.
