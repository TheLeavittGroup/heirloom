Then here's what happens
  • 3
    Inspection day
    I meet the adjuster at your home and show them the damage.
    You don't have to be there if you can't make it. Insurance decides what it covers. Plan for roughly 1-2 weeks after your claim is filed - your insurer confirms the date.
  • 4
    The estimate
    Text or email me the estimate PDF.
    Insurance emails it to you as a PDF, or sometimes texts a link to the cell number they have on file. Can't find it? Just ask your adjuster for it. Allow roughly 1-3 weeks after inspection - it can take longer.
  • 5
    First payment
    Text me as soon as it arrives.
    Insurance mails you a physical check (your lender may need to sign it if you have a mortgage). Your deposit from it lets us order your materials - I'll pick it up, or you can pay online from a link we send - whichever's easier.
  • 6
    Supplement
    We review the estimate and send insurance a supplement for anything it missed. We handle this part.
    It can overlap with your first check. Allow roughly 1-4 weeks - it can take longer.
  • 7
    Start date
    Once insurance approves the full roof replacement and your deposit is in, we confirm the start day.
  • 8
    Install and final payment
    We replace your roof and handle all the final paperwork with insurance.
    Once you're happy and love everything, we collect the final payment.

Suggested planning windows only - your insurer confirms the real timing, and delays can extend these estimates.
